# FOF (Fate of Fighters / Forces of Flame) — Character Roster & Lore Draft v0.1

## Overview
**Fate of Fighters (FOF)** is an epic fantasy tactical role-playing game where ancient elemental deities and mortal champions clash across fractured dimensions to claim the *Crest of Eternity*.

---

## 1. Character Roster (v0.1)

### 1. **Ignis the Cinderblade**
* **Faction**: Crimson Vanguard (Fire)
* **Role**: Frontline Physical Striker / Berserker
* **Base Stats**: HP: 1450 | ATK: 165 | DEF: 110 | SPD: 95 | Crit: 24%
* **Signature Weapon**: *Promethean Greatsword*
* **Abilities**:
  - **Flame Slash** (Active): Deals 180% fire damage to a single enemy, applying Burn (deals 5% max HP damage/turn for 3 turns).
  - **Cinder Fury** (Passive): When HP drops below 40%, ATK increases by +35% and gains 20% lifesteal.
  - **Inferno Cleave** (Ultimate): Slams down blazing shockwave dealing 320% AoE damage and shattering enemy defenses by 30%.
* **Backstory**: Former general of the Ashen Citadel who channeled the forbidden spark of Mount Pyros to protect his besieged homeland.

## 2. Story Plot & Campaign Arcs

### **Prologue: The Shattered Convergence**
The *Crest of Eternity*—the celestial artifact balancing the elemental forces—shatters into 6 Prime Shards across the continent of Aethelgard. Rogue dimensional anomalies spawn corrupted beasts and chaos rifts.

### **Act I: The Ashen Crucible**
Ignis awakens near the burning ruins of the Pyros Valley. Seeking the Flame Shard, he encounters Valeria at the Glacial Border. Initial mistrust sparks a fierce duel before they unite to fend off Abyssal void raiders.

### **Act II: Whispers of the Tempest**
In the cloud-shrouded spires of Windward, Zephyr joins the vanguard after learning that Malakor seeks to consume the Lightning Shard to open the Great Rift.

### **Act III: Eclipse of Dawn**
At the Sunstone Temple, Aurelia holds back the dark tides. The champions battle through waves of shadow minions and face Malakor's corrupt avatars in a multi-stage showdown.

### **Act IV: The Final Ascension**
All 6 Shards resonate at the Apex of Aethelgard. The party must configure their ultimate synergy team to vanquish the Primordial Void Leviathan and restore balance to the realm.

## 3. Recommended Game Mechanics & Enhancements
- **Dynamic Combat System**: Turn-based tactical battle system with action orders, synergy combos, skill cooldowns, status effects (Burn, Freeze, Stun, Curse, Buffs), and ultimate meters.
- **Roster Management & Customization**: Leveling, equipment upgrades, stat allocations, rune sockets, and skill tree unlocks.
- **Story Campaign & Arena Modes**: Story mode with dialogue cutscenes, Boss Challenge Dungeons, and Endless Survival Arena.
- **Google Drive Cloud Save**: Seamless sync of player progress, roster rosters, items, and combat replay logs directly to the user's Google Drive with permission.
- **Tactical Formation Matrix**: 3x2 Grid formation system (Frontline tanks/brawlers, Backline mages/healers/assassins) affecting targeting priority and cover mechanics.
